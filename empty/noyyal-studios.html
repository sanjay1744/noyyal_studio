Research &
Inquiry
Noyyal Studios maintains an active research practice examining how architecture responds to climate, memory, and lived experience in South India.
RS — 01 · Ongoing
Vernacular Cooling in Dravidian Domestic Space
An investigation into pre-industrial passive cooling strategies embedded in Tamil Nadu's traditional courtyard homes — and how they may inform contemporary residential design under warming climates.
Climate Vernacular Tamil Nadu
RS — 02 · Ongoing
Threshold as Architectural Event
A phenomenological study of entry sequences in South Indian homes — from the kolam-marked threshold to the transitional verandah — asking what it means for a building to mark arrival.
Phenomenology Ritual Domesticity
RS — 03 · Published 2023
Light, Shadow, and the Brick Wall
A material study tracing how brick — raw, plastered, exposed — mediates between inside and outside in contemporary residential buildings across Tamil Nadu and Kerala.
Materials Light Brick
RS — 04 · In Progress
Room as Memory: Spatial Autobiography
Collaborating with residents of long-inhabited homes to map how rooms accumulate meaning over generations — studying architecture as a container of personal and collective memory.
Memory Participatory Ethnography

A studio that
asks questions.
Noyyal Studios is an architecture and research practice founded in Chennai. We work primarily on residential architecture and maintain a parallel research program examining how space, climate, and culture shape each other in South India.

The studio takes its name from the Noyyal river — a tributary of the Kaveri — as a reminder that good architecture, like a river, is shaped by what it flows through.

How we work
01
Site before form
Every design begins with deep site reading — sun, wind, view, memory. Form follows this understanding, never precedes it.
02
Material honesty
We work with local materials and express them directly. Brick is brick. Concrete is concrete. Concealment is not sophistication.
03
Slow design
We take on fewer projects to invest more time in each. Architecture built to outlast its architect deserves that patience.
04
Research-led practice
Every built project is also a research opportunity. Questions from the studio inform the page; answers on the page inform the studio.
